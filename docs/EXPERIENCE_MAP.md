# 愛恨 — EXPERIENCE MAP

## 愛恨
### ÀI HÈN
Love / Hate — A Moonlit Digital Journey

---

# Narrative structure

The website is not a collection of disconnected sections.

It is one continuous journey through a world.

The experience begins as a Chinese ink painting, becomes three-dimensional, enters a moonlit garden, passes through architecture and eventually returns to ink.

---

## 01 — THE PAINTING

The visitor sees what appears to be a traditional shanshui painting.

Elements:

- rice-paper atmosphere
- distant mountains
- rivers and water
- fog
- negative space
- restrained movement

Initially the world should almost feel static.

Title:

愛恨

ÀI HÈN

LOVE / HATE

---

## 02 — AWAKENING

Scrolling begins.

The painting slowly reveals depth.

Mountain layers separate through parallax.

Mist moves independently.

Water gains subtle movement.

The transition from 2D to 3D begins.

---

## 03 — MOON GATE

A white traditional garden wall emerges through the mist.

At its center is a circular Moon Gate.

The circle becomes one of the main visual symbols of the project.

Through the gate the visitor sees:

- moonlight
- mountains
- water
- vegetation
- a distant pavilion

---

## 04 — CROSSING

The camera moves through the Moon Gate.

This is the major transition between worlds.

The visual language evolves from:

ink painting

to

moonlit three-dimensional garden.

---

## 05 — GARDEN REVEAL

The complete garden is revealed.

Elements:

- white / pearl moon
- reflective pond
- mist
- rocks
- bamboo
- willow
- restrained vegetation
- distant mountains
- pavilion

This scene should feel spacious and contemplative.

---

## 06 — PAVILION

The camera approaches a single highly polished Chinese pavilion.

Architecture:

- curved dark roof
- restrained dark wood
- pale walls
- lattice windows
- stone foundation
- subtle warm interior light

Only a very small number of lanterns should exist.

---

## 07 — PROJECT CORRIDOR

The visitor moves through a pavilion corridor.

Traditional Chinese windows become frames for portfolio projects.

Each project should feel like another world visible through a window.

Possible projects:

- Bella Durmiente
- Bella Building Lab
- Flutter projects
- Colegio Master
- future work

The portfolio content remains HTML-based and accessible.

---

## 08 — INK / SKILLS

The physical architecture dissolves.

The environment returns gradually toward rice paper and ink.

Skills emerge through restrained ink-inspired motion.

Possible technologies:

- Three.js
- TypeScript
- React
- Flutter
- Python
- Blender
- UI / UX

---

## 09 — MOON COURT

The visitor reaches a small intimate courtyard.

Elements:

- tree
- stone
- shallow water
- moon
- subtle vegetation
- restrained warm light

This is the contact area.

Primary message:

LET'S CREATE SOMETHING.

---

## 10 — RETURN

The camera slowly moves away.

The 3D world begins to flatten again.

The landscape becomes ink.

The experience ends visually where it started.

The structure is circular.
